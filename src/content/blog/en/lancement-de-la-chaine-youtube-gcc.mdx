## Introduction to Active Directory pentesting

During the weeks of May 11 and May 18, [Zleb](https://app.hackthebox.com/users/2182557) and [Cqban](https://app.hackthebox.com/users/490447) hosted two presentations and workshops introducing Active Directory (AD) pentesting. Both sessions were recorded and are available on the [GCC YouTube channel](https://www.youtube.com/@gcc-ensibs)!

### Day 1 - Active Directory, Kerberos, and DACL fundamentals

This first session introduces the core concepts behind Active Directory pentesting:

- Active Directory in brief
- How Kerberos works
- Introduction to DACLs (Discretionary Access Control Lists)
- Presentation of attack techniques (AS-REP Roasting, Kerberoasting, DACL abuse)

You can find the replay and slides here:

🎥 Replay: https://youtu.be/7_0sfsPvFBU

📑 Slides: <a href={slidesDay1}>slides-ad-cm-1.pdf</a>

### Day 2 - Kerberos delegations and related attacks

The second session presents the different Kerberos delegation mechanisms and the most common exploitation techniques:

- Unconstrained Delegation
- Constrained Delegation
- Resource-Based Constrained Delegation (RBCD)
- Attack scenarios and exploitation paths
