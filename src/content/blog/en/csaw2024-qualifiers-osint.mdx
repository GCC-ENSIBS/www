## Authentic Chinese Food

![Authentic Chinese Food challenge prompt](../../../assets/blog/csaw2024-qualifiers-osint/panda-challenge-description.png)

In the top-right corner of the picture, there is a phone number. After a quick Google search, we realize the number is from New York.

We can then search "Panda Express New York" on Google Maps and find the location that matches the picture above.

![Panda Express storefront at 423 Fulton Street](../../../assets/blog/csaw2024-qualifiers-osint/panda-express-storefront.png)

Its address is: 423 Fulton St, Brooklyn, NY 11201

### Healthgrade

On this website, if we enter the restaurant address, its grade is listed as pending. It turns out this was the first part of the flag:

https://a816-health.nyc.gov/ABCEatsRestaurants/#!/Search/50055636

### Year Built

Some websites say the building was built in 1920, but this article about the specific building says it was built in 1931:
https://www.brownstoner.com/architecture/building-of-the-day-423-fulton-street/

### LLC Name

On several real estate websites, if we enter this address, we find that it is owned by BNN FULTON FLUSHING OWNER LCC.
https://a836-pts-access.nyc.gov/care/datalets/datalet.aspx?mode=asmt_tent_2027&UseSearch=no&pin=3001500011&taxyr=2027&LMparent=20

### Flag

```
csawctf{Pending_1931_Bnn_Fulton_Flushing_Owner}
```


## Rickshaw

![Pink rickshaw with an Indian diplomatic plate](../../../assets/blog/csaw2024-qualifiers-osint/rickshaw.png)

For this challenge, we need to find the phone number of the owner of this vehicle.
After searching Google for the first four characters of this license plate, we realize that this rickshaw is owned by the US embassy in India.

https://www.licenseplatemania.com/landenpaginas/indie.htm

We can then search Google for "pink rickshaw US embassy India" and find, among the first results, this Facebook post from the US embassy in India:
https://www.facebook.com/India.usembassy/videos/the-auto-gang-of-the-us-embassy/839571310398639/

We then go to the embassy's Facebook profile and find their phone number.

### Flag

```
csawctf{+91-11-2419-8000}  
```


## Plane spotting

![Plane Spotting challenge prompt](../../../assets/blog/csaw2024-qualifiers-osint/planespot.png)

A Google search about this incident brings us to this article:
https://www.donaldlovesvladimir.com/post/trump-plane-parked-next-to-russian-government-jet-at-dulles

### Registration number US

We learn that the former president is Donald Trump and that his plane is "Trump Force One". A Google search gives us its registration number, N757AF, and we can also easily find that the Russian plane model is IL-96-300.

### Airport code

In the articles above, we also find that the airport where Trump's plane was parked next to the Russian plane was Washington Dulles, whose code is IAD.

### Registration number RUS

After searching a bit more, we cross-check the Russian plane and its registration number:
https://zenn.dev/tkaliiho/articles/3018ddaddfa946

![Russian aircraft with registration RA-96018](../../../assets/blog/csaw2024-qualifiers-osint/ruplane.jpg)

The registration number is RA-96018.

### US city name

The challenge then talks about another Russian plane that flew to the US.

After searching a bit more online, I found this tweet about another Russian plane that was in the US at the same time as the incident:

https://x.com/MenchOsint/status/1806742133069738360?lang=sr

Its registration number is RA-96019, just one digit above the previous one.
I decide to look at its flight history:

https://fr.flightaware.com/live/flight/RA96019/history
![Flight history for aircraft RA-96019](../../../assets/blog/csaw2024-qualifiers-osint/historyflight.png)

This plane was flying in the US at 21:07, so it looks like I am on the right track. Let's have a closer look.
![Aircraft position near Trenton on the map](../../../assets/blog/csaw2024-qualifiers-osint/planecitynear.png)

After looking at the plane's position on Google Maps, we see that the nearest city is Trenton.

We combine all of this information and get the flag!

### Flag

```
csawctf{RA96018_IAD_N757AF_Trenton}
```


## Literally 1984

This was probably the most controversial OSINT challenge of this CTF. Personally, I liked it a lot and found it pretty original.

![Literally 1984 challenge prompt](../../../assets/blog/csaw2024-qualifiers-osint/1984-challenge-description.png)

The description does not give much information, but if we look at the website's HTML code, we see something interesting:

![HTML code showing a zero-width non-joiner character](../../../assets/blog/csaw2024-qualifiers-osint/secret.png)

`&zwnj` is what we see between "name of" and "made a cover" in the HTML.

If we search this sequence of characters online, it brings us to this wiki page:
https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zero-width_non-joiner

I then decided to search YouTube for singing-related content using this sequence:
![YouTube search results for the zero-width non-joiner character](../../../assets/blog/csaw2024-qualifiers-osint/songsearch.png)
 
In the first results, we find a Japanese singing channel that looks like it has no name and goes by the nickname x0o0x_:
![Japanese YouTube singing channel with no visible name](../../../assets/blog/csaw2024-qualifiers-osint/nonamechannel.png)

In the popular videos of this channel, we see a song called Big Brother. Suddenly, the challenge name makes much more sense.
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5lrseDtxX_E

We then look at the composer's name in the video description and translate it into English, which gives us our flag:

### Flag

```
csawctf{Susumu_Hirasawa}
```


## Mystery

![Mystery challenge prompt](../../../assets/blog/csaw2024-qualifiers-osint/mystery-challenge-description.png)

First, we need to look at the band the producer was part of in 1992. After checking his Wikipedia page, we see that the band is called P-Model.

https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Susumu_Hirasawa

After some more searching, we see that the band only released one album in 1992. I searched every song from this album on YouTube, followed by the previous challenge's artist name, x0o0x_.

We quickly find this song covered by x0o0x_ and another artist called Chogakusei.

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mI8KKipWEp4

### Flag

```
csawctf{Chogakusei}
```
